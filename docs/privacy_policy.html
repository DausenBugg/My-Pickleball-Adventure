Privacy Policy

My Pickleball App
Last updated: March 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to My Pickleball App ("we", "our", "us"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By using My Pickleball App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used for account creation, login, and password recovery.
  • Display name — shown to other players in match results, leaderboards, and friend lists.
  • Profile photo (optional) — uploaded by you and visible to other users.

Game Activity

  • Match data — scores, match types (singles/doubles), ranked/casual mode, and match results.
  • Achievement progress — milestones and badges earned through gameplay.
  • Ratings and rankings — Elo-based skill ratings and leaderboard positions.

Technical Data

  • Push notification tokens — used to deliver in-app notifications (match approvals, friend requests, achievements).
  • Device identifiers — collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) for ad personalization.

5. Advertising

My Pickleball App displays banner advertisements powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads.

You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings:
Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored securely on Supabase cloud infrastructure.
  • All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Database access is protected by Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth with industry-standard hashing.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Match history and ratings are retained indefinitely for leaderboard integrity.
  • Push notification tokens are removed when notifications are disabled or on sign out.

8. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data.
  • Update your display name and profile photo via Settings.
  • Delete your account by contacting us (processed within 30 days; anonymized match records may be retained).
  • Opt out of push notifications in app settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads in device settings.

9. Children’s Privacy

My Pickleball App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
