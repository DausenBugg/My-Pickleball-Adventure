# Privacy Policy

**My Pickleball App**
**Last updated: March 1, 2026**

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **My Pickleball App** ("we", "our", "us"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By using My Pickleball App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### Account Information
- **Email address** — used for account creation, login, and password recovery.
- **Display name** — shown to other players in match results, leaderboards, and friend lists.
- **Profile photo** (optional) — uploaded by you and visible to other users.

### Game Activity
- **Match data** — scores, match types (singles/doubles), ranked/casual mode, and match results.
- **Achievement progress** — milestones and badges earned through gameplay.
- **Ratings and rankings** — Elo-based skill ratings and leaderboard positions.

### Technical Data
- **Push notification tokens** — used to deliver in-app notifications (match approvals, friend requests, achievements).
- **Device identifiers** — collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) for ad personalization.

## 5. Advertising

My Pickleball App displays banner advertisements powered by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's advertising settings:

- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

---

## 6. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored securely on **Supabase** cloud infrastructure.
- All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
- Database access is protected by **Row Level Security (RLS)** — users can only access their own data and public information (leaderboards, match history with participants).
- We do not store passwords in plain text; authentication is handled by Supabase Auth with industry-standard hashing.

---

## 7. Data Retention

- Your account data is retained as long as your account is active.
- Match history and ratings are retained indefinitely to maintain leaderboard integrity.
- Push notification tokens are removed when you disable notifications or sign out.

---

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data (visible in your profile and match history).
- **Update** your display name and profile photo at any time via Settings.
- **Delete your account** — contact us at the email below and we will delete your account and associated personal data within 30 days. Note: anonymized match records may be retained for leaderboard consistency.
- **Opt out of push notifications** — toggle notifications off in Settings.
- **Opt out of personalized ads** — use your device's advertising settings.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

My Pickleball App is not intended for children under the age of **13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
